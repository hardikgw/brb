On Tap

What’s pouring
this season.

Every recipe carries the fingerprint of this specific soil — herbs grown right here, fermented in our on-site brewery. We pour from 12 taps rotating with the seasons and harvest.

🏅 denotes an award-winning beer  ·  High-alcohol beers are served in half pints. Check Facebook to see what's on tap today — or better yet, come find out in person.

Lemon Basil Wheat Beer 🏅

Belgian Saison

Saison

Hop Harvest Party Ale

Fresh-Hop Ale · Late Summer/Early Fall

Pale and pleasant with a smooth bitterness. Freshly hopped with over 7 lbs per barrel of our own Cascade hops grown on-site, brewed from bine to kettle in less than 24 hours.

Peter Peter Pumpkin Drinker Porter

Rosemary Orange Amber Ale

Bay Nut Brown Ale

Brown Ale

Holiday Market Ale

Brewed for our Holiday Market, held the first weekend of November — a once-a-year gift from our head brewer. The style is announced during September. Bottles are great for gift bags, hostess gifts, and party favors.

The Ferminator 🏅

Strong Scotch Ale · Served in Half Pints

Chicken Chow Barleywine

American Barleywine · Served in Half Pints

The richest and strongest of all American ales — malty, complex, and intense. Delicious after a short fermentation, and even better the longer it waits.

Backpacker Pale Ale

6.6% ABV · 33 IBU
Pale Ale

A clean, fresh golden beer loaded with flavor. A gentle malt background balances it perfectly into an extremely easy-drinking session beer.

Citra Golden Hop Surprise IPA

6.3% ABV · 68 IBU
New England IPA

Surprising indeed — the powerful, refreshing aroma of grapefruit and stone fruit fills your senses before your first sip. It’s fruity, it’s floral, it’s hoppy, it’s perfection.

Mr. Pat’s Abbey Ale

Belgian Abbey Ale

A malty, Belgian-style ale with hints of caramel, raisin, and plum, plus slight notes of banana and spice from the Belgian yeast.

Shenandoah Sunset Hazy IPA

6.3% ABV · 70 IBU
New England Hazy IPA

Our most popular hazy hits all the benchmarks — perfect hazy apricot color, thick and chewy mouthfeel, and juicy orange and peach flavors.

St. Billie Girl Belgian Single

Belgian Single

The name is a nod to another delicious beer enjoyed by our owner years ago. Very low hop bitterness and malt flavor, very light in color with a low-to-medium body.

Farmwork Rosemary Rye Saison

4% ABV · 20 IBU
Rye Saison

So tasty and thirst quenching, with a hint of rosemary and plenty of bubbles. First brewed for Belgian farm workers who deserved a cold brew after a long, hot day in the fields — but still had to be up early the next morning, hence the low alcohol.

Appalachian Hefeweizen

Hefeweizen

Witbier

Pilsner
