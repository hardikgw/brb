Booking & Rental Terms

A signed contract is required — a 50% non-refundable payment of the total venue fee is due upon signing, with the remaining balance due 30 days before your event. Events booked within 30 days require payment in full at signing. Full-day rentals run 10:00 am – 10:00 pm; you choose your start time, and all events end by 10:00 pm. Rain plans are easy — there’s indoor space for everything.
