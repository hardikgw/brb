Your Wedding Day

Hundreds of weddings.
Every one different.

The Big Barn has hosted hundreds of weddings, and no two have looked alike. From first look to last dance, the whole day happens in one place — no shuttling between ceremony and reception.

The Ceremony

An 18×18 gazebo with chapel seating for 150+, open fields and mountains behind you — or move the vows inside under the trusses. Rain plans are easy: there's indoor space for everything.

The Reception

Two levels and 10,000 square feet of timber-framed barn, a full bar pouring farm-grown beer, custom catering, and on-site coordination — with the mezzanine overlooking your dance floor.

Getting Ready

Separate bride and groom dressing suites to start the day, and 7+ acres of gardens and green space for portraits — golden hour on a farm does half your photographer's work.

Wedding packages are priced by season and day of the week — quiet winter weekdays open each range, and peak-season Saturdays and holiday weekends top it out. Share your date and we’ll quote it exactly. Planning a rehearsal dinner or a smaller gathering? The Mezzanine rents from $250/hr — see Private Events.

Booking & Rental Terms

A signed contract is required — a 50% non-refundable payment of the total venue fee is due upon signing, with the remaining balance due 30 days before your event. Events booked within 30 days require payment in full at signing. Full-day rentals run 10:00 am – 10:00 pm; you choose your start time, and all events end by 10:00 pm. Rain plans are easy — there’s indoor space for everything.
