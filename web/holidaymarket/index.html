The Market

A holiday weekend
made by hand.

The holiday season is just around the corner, and we're excited to invite talented local artisans and makers to be part of the Backroom Brewery Holiday Market, taking place on Saturday, November 14, and Sunday, November 15.

Our Holiday Market will bring together the local community for a festive weekend filled with handcrafted gifts, delicious food, award-winning craft beer, live entertainment, and holiday cheer. It's the perfect opportunity to showcase your products and connect with hundreds of shoppers looking for one-of-a-kind gifts.

Space is limited, and vendor selections will be made to ensure a diverse and unique shopping experience for our guests. Applications are reviewed as they are received — apply early to secure your spot.
